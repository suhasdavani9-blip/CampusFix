Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

CampusFix is dedicated to protecting user privacy while facilitating civil engineering problem reporting across campus.

1. Data Collected

We collect details related to reported infrastructure issues, including problem categories, photos, priority levels, and location descriptions.

2. Purpose of Collection

The collected data is used exclusively by campus maintenance teams and civil engineering authorities to identify, prioritize, and repair facility defects.

3. Data Sharing & Security

Your reports are stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore. No personal data is sold or shared with external third-party advertisers.
